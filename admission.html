How To Apply

Applications for Junior College (FYJC) are processed through the Maharashtra centralised admission process. Degree College (FYBCOM / FYBSC / BBA / BCA / BMS) applicants can apply directly through our Online Application Portal, or visit the college office with SSC / HSC marksheets, leaving certificate, Aadhaar card and passport-size photographs for offline admission.
